## About me

I'm a **final-year Computing student** at the **University of Greenwich (Vietnam)**. I build **computer vision systems** that turn camera footage into useful information, and **backend web applications** with clean APIs, authentication and databases. I enjoy taking an idea from research or a prototype to something that runs reliably, and I learn best by **building real projects**, alone or in a team.

Right now I'm **continuing my research in AI and computer vision**, and I'm working on **new projects around AI topics and applications**: exploring ideas, training and evaluating models, and turning the useful ones into working systems. Alongside that, I keep growing my **fullstack and backend** skills through team projects and my own builds. I like to be **honest about results**, including the limitations, and to keep learning from every project.
